Ken Holstein is a Tenure Track Assistant Professor at EPFL directing the CoALA Lab, researching human-AI complementarity, participatory AI design, and AI augmentation of human expertise across HCI, learning sciences, and responsible AI.

## Changelog

### 2026-10-27

- **New EPFL appointment**: Ken Holstein has joined EPFL as a Tenure Track Assistant Professor (IC/IINFCOM), directing the CoALA Lab; previously held the same rank at CMU.
- **Lab affiliation**: Part of the EPFL–ETHZ CLUE center and CMU-NIST AIMSEC cooperative research center; also Affiliate Professor at CMU.
- **CHI 2026 papers (April 2026)**: Multiple papers accepted, including *Botender* (case-based AI agent co-design), *PolicyPad* (collaborative LLM policy prototyping), *"I Don't Think RAI Applies to My Model"* (responsible AI engagement), *Prototyping Multimodal GenAI Real-Time Agents*, *Funding AI for Good*, and two workshop papers on HCXAI and Tools for Thought.
- **UIST 2025**: *Policy Maps* — tools for guiding unbounded LLM behavior spaces.
- **DIS 2025**: Two papers — *Metacognitive Support Agents for Human-AI Co-creation* and *Making the Right Thing* (Best Paper Honorable Mention).
- **CSCW 2025 Best Paper**: *WeAudit* — scaffolding user auditors and AI practitioners in auditing generative AI.
- **FAccT 2026**: *Toward Third-Party Assurance of AI Systems* (2 citations).
- **Recent arXiv preprints (2026)**: *ComplLLM* (fine-tuning LLMs for complementary decision signals), *PersonaTeaming* (persona-driven red-teaming for GenAI), *Who Does What in AI Auditing?*, *What We Know about Responsible AI Practices in Industry* (half-decade review), and *AI Measurement as a Collaborative Design Practice*.
- **Big Data & Society 2026**: *AI Failure Loops in Devalued Work* — published paper on overconfidence in AI and underconfidence in worker expertise.
- **AIES 2026**: Extended abstract on enabling social workers to design LLM augmentation evaluations.
- **New team members**: Postdoc Anna Sotnikova (NLP+HCI+Learning Sciences) and visiting PhD Yuanhao Zhang joined the lab; PhD student Hayden Stec co-advised with Tanja Käser.
- **Several alumni placements**: Multiple CMU PhD graduates (class of '26) placed at Microsoft Research, Georgia Tech (TT faculty), Duolingo, Microsoft, and NIST/CAISI.
