Thomas Vidick leads the QCC Lab at EPFL, working at the intersection of quantum complexity theory, interactive proof systems, quantum error correction, and the stability of group representations.

## Changelog

### 2026-09-07

- **New preprint (Jul 2026):** "Polynomial Hilbert-Schmidt Stability of the Lamplighter Group" (arXiv:2607.20135, with Alon Dogon) — establishes explicit polynomial bounds on the stability rate and radius of the lamplighter group, giving the first example of an infinitely presented group with an explicit upper bound on the stability radius; techniques draw on approximately invariant measures, descriptive combinatorics, and distributed LOCAL algorithms. This extends Vidick's ongoing research thread on group stability and soficity (connected to the Aldous–Lyons and MIP*=RE lines of work).
- **New PhD student listed:** Petia Arabadjieva now appears alongside Aviv Taller on the EPFL profile page, indicating lab growth.
- No other materially new developments beyond what was recorded in the previous changelog entry (Jun 2026).

### 2026-07-10

- **New preprint (June 2026):** "Probabilistically Checking Quantum Proofs, with Interaction" (arXiv:2606.09588, with Baocheng Sun) — establishes a quantum interactive oracle proof (qIOP) for QMA, providing the first information-theoretically sound, local and robust characterization of QMA without requiring a quantum PCP theorem; the protocol exploits quantum locally testable codes (LTCs) combined with classical PCPP techniques.
- **Recent preprint (Jan 2026):** "Quantum Interactive Oracle Proofs" (arXiv:2601.12874) — also published in LNCS (Dec 2025), formalizing the quantum IOP model.
- **Recent preprint (Oct 2025):** "Derandomised tensor product gap amplification for quantum Hamiltonians" (arXiv:2510.01333).
- **Ongoing series (late 2024–2025):** Two-part work on the Aldous–Lyons Conjecture (arXiv:2408.00110 and arXiv:2501.00173), with Part II establishing undecidability.
- **FOCS 2024:** "Expansion of High-Dimensional Cubical Complexes with Application to Quantum Locally Testable Codes" (4 citations).
- Lab website minimal; only one PhD student (Aviv Taller) listed publicly.
