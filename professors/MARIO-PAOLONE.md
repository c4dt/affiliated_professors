# Mario Paolone

**Lab:** Distributed Electrical Systems Laboratory (DESL)  
**EPFL profile:** [people.epfl.ch](https://people.epfl.ch/mario.paolone)  
**Web:** [desl-pwrs.epfl.ch](https://desl-pwrs.epfl.ch/)  
**ORCID:** [0000-0001-7073-9036](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-7073-9036)  
**OpenAlex:** [A5050504092](https://openalex.org/A5050504092)  

Mario Paolone leads EPFL's Distributed Electrical Systems Laboratory (DESL), advancing smart grid technologies including PMU-based monitoring of active distribution networks, optimal planning of distributed energy resources, EV integration, carbon flow tracing, and multi-energy system co-planning toward net-zero.

- **New paper – Pelton turbine start-up optimization**: "Optimization of Pelton turbines start-up considering fatigue damage via a fracture mechanics model" (*International Journal of Fatigue*, Aug 2025) — interdisciplinary output linked to DESL's hydraulic machines platform involvement.

- **New paper – Lausanne LV grid planning**: "Planning of the Lausanne low voltage grid via probabilistic load flow analysis" (*IET conference proceedings*, Oct 2025), applying probabilistic methods to a real local network case study.

- **New paper – EV charging station sizing**: "Optimal Sizing of Battery and Grid Connection of Electric Vehicle Charging Stations" (*IEEE PowerTech*, Jun 2025).
- **New paper – AC-OPF for EV/PV joint siting**: "AC-OPF-based Joint Optimal Sizing and Siting of EV Charging Stations and PV Plants in Coupled Traffic-Power Networks" (*IEEE PowerTech*, Jun 2025).
- **New paper – ESS allocation via DLMP**: "Optimal Energy Storage Systems Allocation in Distribution Grids using Decomposed Distribution Locational Marginal Pricing" (*IEEE PowerTech*, Jun 2025).
