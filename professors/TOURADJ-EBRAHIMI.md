# Touradj Ebrahimi

**Lab:** Multimedia Signal Processing Group (MMSPG)  
**EPFL profile:** [people.epfl.ch](https://people.epfl.ch/touradj.ebrahimi)  
**Web:** [mmspg.epfl.ch](https://mmspg.epfl.ch/)  
**ORCID:** [0000-0002-9900-3687](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-9900-3687)

Touradj Ebrahimi leads EPFL's Multimedia Signal Processing Group (MMSPG), focusing on AI-powered image/video coding, JPEG standardization (JPEG AI, JPEG Trust, JPEG DNA), media security, and fine-grained quality assessment.

## Changelog

### 2026-09-08

- **JPEG AIC2026 dataset released** (arXiv, July 2026): A large-scale benchmark dataset for fine-grained assessment of image coding, covering 70 source images, 12 codecs (8 conventional + 4 learning-based), and 9,618 distorted images at perceptually spaced distortion levels. The dataset is publicly available and co-authored with JPEG standardization partners.
- **JPEG Trust at ICIP 2026**: A new paper ("Assessing Media Authenticity Through Watermarking in the Context of the JPEG Trust Standard") was presented at ICIP 2026 (August 2026), extending the JPEG Trust standardization work into watermarking-based authenticity assessment.
- **DNA-based image storage at PCS 2025** (December 2025): New paper on substitution and indel error correction for DNA-based image storage.
- **JPEG AI citation count up**: The flagship JPEG AI IEEE Multimedia paper (Oct 2024) has now reached 25 citations (up from 21 at last profile).
- No new PhD students or major lab restructuring noted.

### 2026-07-10

- **First profile created** for Prof. Touradj Ebrahimi (MMSPG, EPFL).
- **JPEG AI standard published**: A landmark IEEE Multimedia paper (Oct 2024, 21 citations) describes JPEG AI as the first international standard for end-to-end learning-based image coding.
- **JPEG DNA active research front**: Multiple 2024–2025 papers cover DNA-based image storage with error correction (substitution/indel), fountain codes for point clouds, and learning-based source coding for JPEG DNA.
- **Fine-grained image/HDR quality assessment**: New methodology papers at DCC 2025, QoMEX 2025, and EUVIP 2025 introduce fine-grained subjective quality metrics for high-fidelity and HDR images.
- **Trustworthy AI imaging**: A 2025 SPIE paper explores trustworthy AI-powered image processing for Earth observation and cultural heritage applications.
- **Generative AI for advertising**: New work on cost-effective synthetic image generation for advertisement use cases (SPIE 2025).
- **Pose-invariant face recognition**: Presented at ICIP 2025 (2 citations already).
- **Learning-based event data compression**: Lossless compression for computer vision applications presented at SPIE 2025.
- **JPEG Status Report 2024** published in SMPTE Motion Imaging Journal (Nov 2024).
- Prof. Ebrahimi is Convenor of the JPEG standardization committee; one current PhD student: Enes Eray Demirtas.
